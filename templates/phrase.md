# Phrase mode

Use one compact card per requested phrase. For an already-clear input, retain it and briefly explain why; still disclose any relevant evidence issue.

**Original:** exact permitted wording, with neutral privacy redactions when needed.
**May be heard as:** plausible comprehension gap, explicitly labeled as an inference unless actual feedback supports it.
**Suggested wording:** faithful, usable replacement. Preserve actor, action, tense, scope, and commitment. No new activity hidden inside it.
**Why:** the business question it makes clearer.
**Evidence/condition:** only the material qualification or missing fact needed. Do not recite all policy rules.
**Optional recommendation:** include only when useful, separately labeled as new and not already agreed.

If the phrase is misleading, provide a qualified alternative and explain what changed. If it is too incomplete to translate faithfully, give the supported portion and one exact question. Do not ask for a strategy brief to translate a simple term.
