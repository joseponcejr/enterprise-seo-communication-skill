# Page communication audit mode

## Scope and verdict

State which URL or supplied excerpt and sections were actually inspected, audience, and any gaps. Give a short communication verdict, not an invented score or measured SEO/conversion result. If nothing was accessible, stop at the access limitation and request usable text.

## Prioritized wording findings

For each material issue:
- **Location and original:** exact permitted wording and heading/locator.
- **Likely reception:** explicitly an inference unless feedback supports it.
- **Suggested replacement:** faithful wording, with unsupported assertions explicitly qualified.
- **Why it matters:** specific reader question answered.
- **Evidence condition:** claim or scope that needs confirmation.
- **Priority:** high / medium / low, with a reason.

High: misleading claim, material risk, unclear core offer or next step blocking a buyer decision.
Medium: missing explanation of fit/proof/process that hinders evaluation.
Low: local jargon or unnecessary wording that does not change the decision.

These priorities are communication judgments, not numerical performance scores. Do not force one finding per section. Preserve already-clear language.

## Missing business explanations

List relevant gaps separately from observed defects. If the excerpt omits proof, say proof was not supplied in the inspected excerpt, not that the company has none. Suggest a concrete type of evidence or wording template; do not fabricate testimonials, results, audience fit, process steps, or guarantees.

## Optional next action

The smallest useful proposed improvement and how the owner could verify it. No implementation, publishing, or new strategic priority is implied.
