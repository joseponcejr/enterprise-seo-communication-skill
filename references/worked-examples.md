# Worked examples

All examples below are fictional teaching material, not measured results or completed client work. Reuse the reasoning, never the example facts. Wording is illustrative; other accurate rewrites can pass.

## Phrase: explain the work without expanding it

**Input:** We are migrating Yoast to RankMath.

**Original:** We are migrating Yoast to RankMath.
**May be heard as (inference):** A software change without a clear description of what is moving.
**Suggested wording:** We are moving the site's SEO configuration from Yoast to RankMath.
**Why:** Names the work and keeps it in progress.
**Evidence/condition:** This does not establish the number of installations, completion, or measured impact.
**Optional recommendation:** If not already in scope, agree on which settings must be checked after migration. This is a proposed check, not a new commitment inside the translation.

## Phrase: already clear

**Input:** We corrected the page titles on the three priority service pages. Performance will be reviewed next month.

**Suggested wording:** Retain the original.
**Why:** It states what changed, the scope, and when performance will be reviewed. Do not add results that have not been measured or relabel three pages as three sites.

## Phrase: safe qualification

**Input:** Our AI citations prove that buyers prefer us.

**Suggested wording:** Our content is being cited in AI answers. That does not establish buyer preference.
**Why:** Separates a visibility observation from an unsupported customer claim.
**Evidence/condition:** If the citation observation itself is only reported, say “The report says our content is being cited.” Retain actual sample and period where supplied.
**Optional recommendation:** Use buyer or conversion evidence to assess preference. Do not invent a survey result.

## Document conversion: full positive example

### Supplied input

Audience: marketing director. Scope: complete three-section fictional report, client-shareable. The named source export is supplied as context, not independently accessed by the translator.

D1. Search performance. The October export for the English service section shows 1,200 non-brand organic clicks, compared with 1,000 in September. The team suspects an article refresh helped. Attribution was not tested.

D2. Technical work. The team corrected canonical tags on four service pages and verified those tags. No traffic impact has been measured for that fix. Migration to a new SEO plugin is still proposed, not approved.

D3. Next decision. The marketing director is asked to approve reviewing the two remaining service pages next month. Implementation is not included in that request.

### Clean executive draft

**More non-brand search traffic; the cause is not yet established.** The supplied October export shows 1,200 non-brand organic clicks to the English service section, compared with 1,000 in September. The article refresh may have contributed, but attribution has not been tested.

The team corrected and checked the canonical tags that identify the preferred pages on four service pages. No traffic impact from that fix has been measured. The SEO-plugin migration remains a proposal and has not been approved.

**Decision requested:** approve a review of the two remaining service pages next month. This request does not include implementation.

### Change and evidence record

- C1, D1: supplied-export observation, click values/period/English service scope retained in the opening paragraph. No new growth percentage calculated.
- C2, D1: hypothesis about the refresh stays a possible contributor; untested attribution remains explicit.
- C3, D2: completed and verified tag correction stays completed; no traffic recovery claimed.
- C4, D2: migration remains proposed and unapproved.
- C5, D3: approval request retains two-page review scope, next month, and no implementation.

### Coverage

D1 rewritten, D2 rewritten, D3 retained in plainer wording. Complete coverage of the supplied three-section text only. No independent access to the original export is claimed. No missing input blocks this rewrite.

## Page communication audit: full positive example

### Supplied input

Complete supplied page excerpt, not verified as the complete website. Audience: enterprise buyers.

P1, hero: “We implement semantic triples and entity reconciliation for B2B software companies.”
P2, benefit: “We guarantee your brand will become the number one AI recommendation.”
P3, process: “We assess your current pages and provide a prioritized list of wording changes. Your team approves and publishes the changes.”
P4, CTA: “Request a page assessment.”

### Audit output

**Scope:** P1–P4 of the supplied excerpt. No live-site or technical inspection.
**Verdict:** The process and next step are clear, but the opening is technical and the guarantee overstates what the service can control.

**High priority, P2**
- Original: “We guarantee your brand will become the number one AI recommendation.”
- Likely reception (inference): a promise of a specific AI outcome.
- Replacement: “We identify wording changes that make your offer and business facts clearer.”
- Why: describes the supplied assessment service rather than promising a ranking outcome.
- Condition: this intentionally removes an unsupported guarantee. Do not use it to imply that AI recommendation performance was tested.

**Medium priority, P1**
- Original: “We implement semantic triples and entity reconciliation for B2B software companies.”
- Likely reception (inference): unclear what the work changes for the buyer.
- Replacement: “We clarify the business facts and relationships on B2B software websites.”
- Why: explains the action and retains the stated audience.
- Condition: retain the technical terms in implementation detail where needed; this does not promise results or change who publishes.

**Retain P3 and P4:** The process, responsibility, and next step already make sense. Do not invent a booking workflow or add a delivery deadline.

**Missing business explanation:** The excerpt supplies no example assessment or proof of the team's work. Consider an approved sample or documented before/after wording example. This is a gap in the inspected excerpt, not a claim that the company has no evidence.

**Optional next action:** Replace the unsupported guarantee and clarify the hero; keep the existing process and CTA. The owner still approves and publishes.

## Partial-input response

If an extraction states “pages 1–2 of 8, remaining pages unavailable,” start with: “I can convert the supplied pages 1–2. This is a partial review; pages 3–8 were not inspected.” Provide the useful rewrite and account for those two pages. Request the missing pages only if a complete-document result is needed. Do not fill them with plausible content.
